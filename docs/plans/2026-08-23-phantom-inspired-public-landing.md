# Phantom-inspired public landing direction

**Status:** Approved for public landing only
**Reference:** Phantom Wallet homepage, inspected 2026-08-23. Borrow confidence, scale, atmosphere, and product staging. Do not copy Phantom typography, ghost identity, pill language, card radius, or product claims.

**Phase boundary:** This follow-up starts after the broader calm-precision rebrand in `2026-08-22-calm-precision-rebrand.md`. “Authenticated app styling remains unchanged” means this landing phase adds no further app/auth styling changes; preceding rebrand work remains a separate atomic commit.

## Design thesis

Archimedes should feel like a specific instrument, not a generated fintech template. Replace repeated mono kickers, evenly ruled registers, and decorative numbering with fewer, larger statements. Keep verified content and real product capture.

## Tokens

- Haze `#EFEDFF`: dominant light atmosphere
- Paper `#FFFCF6`: evidence surfaces
- Ink `#17151F`: primary text and product theatre
- Stage `#0C0C11`: hero and ownership bands
- Signal `#625CF6`: sole decorative/action accent
- Proof `#147A69`: semantic verification only

Dark mode maps haze to `#15131D`, paper to `#211E2A`, signal to `#A79EFF`, and preserves readable warm-white text. No gradients.

## Typography

Self-host OFL-licensed **Gabarito** for display/body and **IBM Plex Mono** for evidence/data. Headlines use weight 520–620, tight but not crushed tracking, and deliberate two-to-three-line wraps. Mono appears only where content is genuinely machine-readable.

## Composition

```text
[quiet header]
[near-black product theatre: thesis + CTA + real workspace]
[three plain proof statements]
[problem statement | sticky four-check proof deck]
[capabilities folded into one five-stage accountable path]
[three offset use-case scenes + named rails]
[full-width ownership cut: agent may / only you may]
[FAQ]
[direct final action + compact footer]
```

Signature: **proof deck**. Four independent rigor checks overlap as physical evidence sheets on desktop using CSS sticky positioning; mobile receives a normal readable stack. Motion stays limited to one short hero reveal and native sticky progression. Cards remain at or below 10px radius.

## Acceptance

Preserve every public anchor, API-backed contract count, testnet disclaimer, wallet boundary, theme, keyboard path, reduced motion, and 390px overflow guarantee. Maintain WCAG AA contrast and 44px touch targets. Authenticated app styling remains unchanged.
